TuiTimeFormat
[tuiTimeFormat]="{dayPeriod, separators}"
Tuple of two locale-specific day-period markers (AM-marker first, PM-marker second). When both are non-empty, the mask switches to 12-hour format and appends the selected marker to the time.

Default: ['', ''] (24-hour format)

Separators between time segments (from left to right). If there are fewer separators than positions in the time mode, the remaining positions use the canonical separators of the mode ( ':' or '.' ).

Default: [] (canonical separators of the mode)
