TuiSearchBar is a mobile search field. Put tuiSearchBar on a <form> or a <search> element, project a native <input tuiSearchBar> into it and a <button tuiIconButton tuiButtonClose> next to it. The searchbar only lays them out per platform: on iOS the button sits to the right of the field, on Android it moves inside the field on the leading side and turns into a back arrow.

No form here, so the cancel button clears the field through a FormControl An active field on iOS is floating : liquid glass with a round cancel button. The bar sits on a <form> , so clearing and Enter work on their own Pinned along with the app bar, the field is floating from the start and keeps that look while the content scrolls under it. Pinning is up to you, the search bar only takes care of the look. Once the field becomes active, it takes the place of the app bar Under a large heading the field starts inline and turns floating once the heading scrolls away — here the switch is driven by an IntersectionObserver watching the heading On iOS with liquid glass, a progressive blur can be shown under the pinned field once the content scrolls beneath it. To activate it, apply the tui-liquid-glass_blur class to the tuiSearchBar element. If the background is colored, provide it via the --tui-progressive-blur-background CSS variable for correct fading
